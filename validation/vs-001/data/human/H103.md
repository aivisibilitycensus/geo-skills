# The 10 Best Receipt Scanners for Effortless Organization

Whether you're managing your [small business'](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/how-to-start-a-business?_ga=2.240183232.294769258.1539010043-215345474.1536196549) finances or filing taxes, it's necessary to know where your money is being spent. But receipts from your purchases are likely to get lost in a sea of documents and miscellaneous papers.

If you struggle to keep track of your receipts, a receipt scanner can come in handy. Some receipt scanners include online tools that allow you to access your receipts from anywhere, so receipts will be consolidated and you can access them whenever you need them.

There are different types of receipt scanners (e.g. desktop and portable) and receipt scanning apps, and I've compiled a list of the best tools to manage your receipts.

Ready to find a receipt scanner to help you get organized? Let's begin.

### 1\. [Expensify](https://www.expensify.com/)

**Best for:** An all-around app

Expensify is a cloud-based app for both iOS and Android. It's a receipt management tool that connects with Expensify tools for expense reporting. You can use your smartphone camera to take a photo of the receipt, the app will automatically pull out the charges. From there, you can associate the charge with an expense report.

Keep in mind that sometimes the receipt scanner is unable to perfectly capture the details of your receipt. Be sure to double check the expenses to make sure the receipt charges are added accurately.

### 2\. [Receipts by Wave](https://www.waveapps.com/receipts/)

**Best for:** Freelancers and small business owners

Receipts by Wave is a receipt tracking app that can be used by small businesses to stay on top of their receipts. It syncs with their cloud-based expense reporting software that can be accessed both on mobile and from your computer. One main perk of this tool is that you can use it for free -- making this receipt scanner option ideal for small businesses and [freelancers](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/freelancing?_ga=2.191440490.1569269847.1539026453-54427254.1534474280) just starting out.

Your data can sync with the [Wave Accounting](https://www.waveapps.com/accounting) app so you can stay on top of your spending. You can also export the data to a variety of accounting tools, such as QuickBooks and Excel, if need be.

### 3\. [Neat](https://www.neat.com/solutions/mobile-receipt-scanning-app/)

**Best for:** Team collaboration

If you need to share your expenses with a team of people or have them reviewed by your manager, this app is for you. Its mobile app is available for iOS and Android users, and your scans are synced across devices.

Use the tool to snap a photo of your receipt, and it's able to recognize the text and gather the details of your receipt. From there, you can create expense reports and even itemized lists.

### 4\. [Evernote Scannable](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/evernote-scannable/id883338188?mt=8)

**Best for:** iOS users

If you're an iPhone or iPad user, this app is for you. You can take a photo of your receipts and they'll be added to your Evernote account. After taking a photo for your receipt, you have the ability to preview the scan, discard, and retake the photo if the first image isn't clear enough.

The receipts will be saved to Evernote's cloud-based app, and can be accessed online from anywhere.

### 5\. [Shoeboxed](https://www.shoeboxed.com/)

**Best for:** Tax season

The Shoeboxed app allows you to scan receipts and keep track of your mileage for tax reporting purposes. It also gives you the ability to specify which expenses are deductible -- making it the ideal tool for tax time.

Create expense reports directly within the app and send them to your colleagues or tax preparer right from your phone.

### 6\. [Veryfi Core](https://veryfi.app/core/)

**Best for:** Data privacy

If you deal with sensitive customer data, such as medical information, this tool is [HIPAA compliant](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/index.html). Veryfi Core gives you the ability to automate data entry of receipts and expenses. It also automates tax coding or categorization.

Your data can be accessed from anywhere, and you can collaborate with your team from anywhere at any time.

### 7\. [Smart Receipts](https://www.smartreceipts.co/)

**Best for:** Custom reports

Once you've scanned your receipts into Smart Receipts, you can create customizable PDF, CSV, and ZIP reports. It also comes with 20 different default data types (e.g. dates, price, receipt categories, payment methods, and more.)

Another unique feature of Smart Receipts is its white labeling services. With these services, your company can create a customized expense reporting app for your users or customers.

### 8\. [Vupoint ST470](https://www.vupointsolutions.com/magic-wand)

**Best for:** An on-the-go, portable scanner

One of the best features of this scanner is its 2-in-1 capability and portability. It functions as a desktop scanner, but it also has a detachable wand you can use to scan. This wand is the perfect size to take with you on the go, and it even comes with its own carrying case.

While the Vupoint ST470 doesn't give you the ability to connect to cloud services, it does have a micro SD card you can save your receipts to for on-the-go access.

### 9\. [Fujitsu ScanSnap IX500](http://www.fujitsu.com/us/products/computing/peripheral/scanners/scansnap/ix500/)

**Best for:** A portable scanner that connects with cloud-based apps

This is a desktop receipt scanner, but it's small enough to travel with. There's also a wireless option, and your receipts can be uploaded into PDF, Excel, and JPEG formats.

Your data is searchable once it's uploaded. Additionally, it can be integrated with cloud-based tools, like [QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/online/), making it easier to manage your accounting.

### 10\. [Epson Workforce DS-30](https://epson.com/Support/Scanners/WorkForce-Series/Epson-WorkForce-DS-30/s/SPT_B11B206201)

**Best for:** A multi-purpose portable scanner

The Epson Workforce DS-30 is a lightweight, portable scanner that allows you to scan receipts and other documents. It has auto-size detection to determine if you're scanning a small receipt or a full-size document.

It works with Windows and Mac computers via USB. All of the text from your scans is searchable, and the files can be uploaded to your computer and cloud-based tools for easy access.
