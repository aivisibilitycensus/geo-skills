# What is Customer Success?

If you've been researching software solutions or business services lately, you've probably noticed companies talking a lot about "customer success." But what exactly does this term mean, and more importantly, why should it matter to you as a potential customer?

Customer success has evolved from a buzzword into a fundamental business philosophy that's changing how companies interact with their clients. In this article, we'll explore what customer success really means, how it differs from traditional customer service, and what you can expect from a company that prioritizes your success.

## Defining Customer Success

At its core, customer success is a proactive, long-term approach to ensuring that customers achieve their desired outcomes while using a company's product or service. Unlike traditional customer support, which typically responds to problems after they occur, customer success focuses on helping you reach your goals before issues arise.

Think of it this way: customer success means your vendor actively works to ensure you're getting maximum value from your investment. They're not just waiting for you to call with a problem—they're reaching out to help you succeed, offering guidance, sharing best practices, and ensuring you're on the right path from day one.

This approach represents a fundamental shift in the customer-vendor relationship. Instead of a transactional interaction that ends when you make a purchase, customer success creates an ongoing partnership where your vendor has a vested interest in your achievements.

## How Customer Success Differs from Customer Support

While customer success and customer support both focus on the customer experience, they serve distinctly different purposes in your journey with a company.

**Customer support** is reactive and problem-focused. When something breaks or you can't figure out how to complete a task, you reach out to customer support for help. They're there to troubleshoot issues, answer questions, and resolve problems. This is essential, but it's essentially a "break-fix" relationship.

**Customer success**, on the other hand, is proactive and goal-focused. A customer success team works with you to understand your business objectives and helps you leverage the product or service to achieve those goals. They monitor your usage, identify opportunities for improvement, and provide strategic guidance tailored to your specific needs.

For example, a customer support team might help you reset your password or troubleshoot a technical issue. A customer success team would check in quarterly to review how you're using the platform, suggest features you're not utilizing that could benefit your business, and share case studies of how similar companies achieved results.

## The Key Components of Customer Success

Understanding what customer success entails can help you evaluate potential vendors and know what to expect from companies that embrace this philosophy. Here are the essential elements:

### Onboarding and Implementation

Customer success begins the moment you decide to work with a company. A robust onboarding process ensures you understand how to use the product effectively from the start. This might include personalized training sessions, implementation support, and resources tailored to your specific use case.

The goal isn't just to get you up and running—it's to set you up for long-term success. Your customer success team should work to understand your objectives and configure the solution to best meet your needs.

### Regular Check-ins and Communication

Proactive communication is a hallmark of customer success. Rather than waiting for you to encounter problems, your customer success manager should regularly reach out to discuss your experience, review your progress toward goals, and identify any challenges you're facing.

These check-ins might be monthly, quarterly, or based on key milestones in your journey. The frequency should match your needs and the complexity of the solution.

### Performance Monitoring and Analytics

A strong customer success program includes tracking how you're using the product and measuring the value you're receiving. Your customer success team should help you establish relevant metrics and regularly review them with you.

This data-driven approach ensures you're getting return on your investment and allows for course corrections if certain features or strategies aren't delivering the expected results.

### Strategic Guidance and Best Practices

Your customer success team serves as a trusted advisor, bringing insights from across their customer base. They should share best practices, industry trends, and innovative ways to use the product that you might not have considered.

This expertise can be invaluable, especially as your needs evolve or as the product introduces new features and capabilities.

## The Benefits of Customer Success for You

Now that you understand what customer success is, let's talk about why it matters for your business.

### Faster Time to Value

With dedicated support from a customer success team, you'll start seeing results more quickly. Rather than figuring everything out through trial and error, you benefit from structured guidance and proven strategies that accelerate your path to success.

### Higher Return on Investment

Customer success ensures you're maximizing the value of your purchase. By helping you utilize all relevant features and optimize your approach, your customer success team helps you get more from what you've invested.

### Reduced Risk

Making the wrong choice on a business solution can be costly. Customer success reduces this risk by ensuring ongoing alignment between the product and your needs. If your requirements change or new challenges emerge, your customer success team can help you adapt rather than leaving you to struggle on your own.

### Partnership, Not Just a Transaction

Perhaps most importantly, customer success creates a true partnership. Your vendor becomes invested in your outcomes, not just their sale. This alignment of interests means they're motivated to ensure you succeed—because your success drives their success.

## What to Look for in a Customer Success Program

As you evaluate potential vendors, here are some questions to ask about their customer success approach:

- Will you be assigned a dedicated customer success manager?
- What does the onboarding process look like?
- How frequently will you communicate with the customer success team?
- What metrics do they track to measure customer success?
- Can they provide examples of how they've helped similar customers achieve their goals?
- What resources are available (training, documentation, community forums, etc.)?

Companies with mature customer success programs should be able to answer these questions clearly and provide specific examples of how they've driven customer outcomes.

## The Bottom Line

Customer success represents a win-win approach to business relationships. For you as a customer, it means working with a vendor who's genuinely invested in your achievements and committed to helping you get maximum value from your investment.

As you evaluate solutions for your business, consider the customer success capabilities of potential vendors. A strong customer success program can be just as important as the features of the product itself—because even the best solution won't deliver results if you can't implement it effectively or leverage it fully.

Look for companies that view customer success as a core value, not just a department. When you partner with a vendor who prioritizes your success, you're not just buying a product or service—you're gaining an ally committed to helping your business thrive.
