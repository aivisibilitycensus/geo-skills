# JOEY Restaurants reduces time to interview by 60% with Workable

Finding the right talent in the hospitality industry has always been challenging. High turnover rates, seasonal hiring spikes, and the need to fill positions quickly while maintaining quality standards create a perfect storm for restaurant recruiters. JOEY Restaurants, a premium casual dining chain with locations across North America, faced these exact challenges—until they discovered how the right recruiting technology could transform their hiring process.

## The challenge: Scaling quality hiring across multiple locations

JOEY Restaurants has built its reputation on delivering exceptional dining experiences. With over 27 locations and growing, the company needed to hire hundreds of team members annually while maintaining the high standards that define their brand. From servers and line cooks to managers and bartenders, each position required careful evaluation to ensure cultural fit and skill alignment.

Before implementing Workable, JOEY's recruiting team faced several critical obstacles:

- **Manual coordination chaos**: Scheduling interviews across multiple locations involved countless emails and phone calls, creating bottlenecks and delays
- **Inconsistent candidate experience**: Without a centralized system, communication gaps left candidates waiting for responses, sometimes for days
- **Limited visibility**: Hiring managers couldn't easily track where candidates were in the pipeline, leading to duplicated efforts and lost opportunities
- **Time-intensive processes**: Recruiters spent hours on administrative tasks instead of building relationships with top talent

These inefficiencies didn't just frustrate the recruiting team—they impacted the business. Open positions remained unfilled longer than necessary, putting pressure on existing staff and potentially affecting customer service quality.

## The solution: Streamlined recruiting with Workable

JOEY Restaurants needed a recruiting platform that could keep pace with their growth while simplifying the hiring workflow. After evaluating several options, they chose Workable for its intuitive interface, robust features, and ability to centralize their entire recruiting operation.

The implementation focused on three key areas:

**Centralized candidate management**: Every application, resume, and communication now flows through a single platform accessible to all stakeholders. Hiring managers across different locations can view candidate profiles, leave feedback, and track progress in real-time.

**Automated scheduling**: One of Workable's most impactful features for JOEY was automated interview scheduling. Instead of the back-and-forth typically required to find mutually convenient times, candidates could select available slots directly, and calendar invitations were sent automatically.

**Mobile accessibility**: In the fast-paced restaurant environment, hiring managers aren't always at their desks. Workable's mobile app enabled them to review candidates, provide feedback, and move the process forward from anywhere—whether they were on the floor during service or between shifts.

## The results: 60% faster time to interview

The impact was immediate and measurable. JOEY Restaurants reduced their time to interview by an impressive 60%, fundamentally changing how quickly they could engage with top talent.

This dramatic improvement stemmed from several factors:

**Elimination of scheduling friction**: What once took an average of five days—sending availability requests, waiting for responses, coordinating between multiple parties—now happened in hours. Candidates could book their own interview slots within minutes of receiving an invitation.

**Faster internal communication**: Hiring managers no longer needed to wait for email updates or weekly meetings to discuss candidates. They could review applications, leave notes, and make decisions within Workable, keeping the process moving continuously.

**Reduced drop-off rates**: In competitive hiring markets, speed matters. By cutting the time between application and interview from over a week to just 2-3 days, JOEY significantly reduced candidate drop-off. Top talent wasn't being snatched up by competitors while waiting for interview scheduling.

Beyond the headline metric, JOEY experienced additional benefits that strengthened their overall recruiting function:

- **Improved candidate satisfaction**: Faster response times and clear communication created a better experience for applicants, enhancing JOEY's employer brand
- **Better quality of hire**: With more time freed from administrative tasks, recruiters could focus on sourcing strategies and candidate engagement, ultimately improving the quality of hires
- **Data-driven decisions**: Workable's reporting features gave leadership visibility into recruiting metrics, enabling them to identify bottlenecks and optimize continuously

## Key features that made the difference

Several specific Workable capabilities proved particularly valuable for JOEY Restaurants:

**Interview kits and scorecards**: Standardized interview questions and evaluation criteria ensured consistency across locations. Every candidate for a server position, for example, was assessed using the same framework, regardless of which restaurant they'd join.

**Pipeline stages**: Custom pipeline stages aligned with JOEY's hiring workflow, giving everyone clear visibility into where each candidate stood and what needed to happen next.

**Integration with job boards**: Posting positions to multiple job boards simultaneously—and pulling all applications into one system—saved hours previously spent managing separate platforms.

**Collaborative hiring**: Multiple team members could be involved in the hiring decision without confusion. Interview feedback, ratings, and notes were all visible to relevant stakeholders, facilitating better-informed hiring decisions.

## Lessons for other multi-location businesses

JOEY Restaurants' success with Workable offers valuable insights for other businesses facing similar recruiting challenges:

**Invest in automation strategically**: Not every process needs a human touch. Identifying high-friction, low-value tasks—like interview scheduling—and automating them frees your team to focus on relationship-building and assessment.

**Prioritize candidate experience**: In tight labor markets, candidates have choices. A smooth, professional recruiting process doesn't just fill positions faster—it becomes a competitive advantage that attracts better talent.

**Get buy-in across locations**: For multi-location businesses, success requires adoption by hiring managers at every site. JOEY's implementation included training and change management to ensure everyone understood the system's value.

**Measure what matters**: Tracking metrics like time to interview, time to hire, and candidate source effectiveness enables continuous improvement and helps justify technology investments.

## The bottom line: Better hires, faster

For JOEY Restaurants, reducing time to interview by 60% wasn't just about efficiency—it fundamentally improved their ability to compete for talent and maintain the service quality their guests expect.

In an industry where every open position affects team morale and customer experience, getting the right people in place quickly matters enormously. By eliminating administrative friction and creating a seamless recruiting workflow, JOEY transformed hiring from a necessary challenge into a strategic advantage.

If your organization is struggling with recruiting coordination, experiencing long time-to-hire metrics, or losing candidates to competitors during lengthy processes, the solution might be simpler than you think. The right recruiting platform can deliver transformative results—just ask JOEY Restaurants.
