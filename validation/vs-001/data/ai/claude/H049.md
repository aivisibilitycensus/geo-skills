# How to Automate Your Small Business in 2019

Running a small business means wearing multiple hats—and often all at once. Between managing employees, serving customers, handling finances, and planning for growth, there simply aren't enough hours in the day. The good news? Many of the repetitive tasks eating up your schedule can be automated, freeing you to focus on what really matters: growing your business and serving your customers.

In this guide, we'll walk you through the essential areas where automation can transform your small business operations, saving you time, reducing errors, and often cutting costs in the process.

## Why Automation Matters for Small Businesses

Before diving into the how, let's address the why. Many small business owners resist automation, thinking it's too expensive, too complicated, or only for larger companies. The reality is quite different.

Automation helps you:

- **Reclaim your time**: Stop spending hours on repetitive tasks and focus on strategic growth
- **Reduce human error**: Automated systems don't forget steps or make calculation mistakes
- **Improve consistency**: Deliver the same quality experience every time, automatically
- **Scale efficiently**: Handle more customers and transactions without proportionally increasing your workload
- **Save money**: While there's an upfront investment, automation typically pays for itself quickly through time savings and error reduction

The key is starting with the right processes—those that are repetitive, time-consuming, and rule-based.

## Automating Your Bookkeeping and Accounting

Let's start with everyone's least favorite task: bookkeeping. If you're still manually entering transactions, categorizing expenses, and wrestling with spreadsheets, you're spending precious time on work that can be almost entirely automated.

**Connect your bank accounts**: Modern accounting solutions automatically import your transactions daily. No more manual entry, no more downloading CSV files, and no more reconciliation headaches. Your transactions flow directly into your accounting system where they can be categorized and tracked.

**Automate transaction categorization**: Smart bookkeeping platforms learn how to categorize your regular expenses automatically. That monthly software subscription? Automatically categorized. Your weekly inventory purchase? Handled without your input. Over time, the system gets smarter about your specific business patterns.

**Generate reports automatically**: Need to see your profit and loss statement? With automated bookkeeping, your financial reports are always up-to-date and available at a click. No more waiting until month-end or scrambling to prepare for investor meetings.

**Simplify tax preparation**: When your bookkeeping is automated and up-to-date, tax time becomes dramatically easier. Your financial data is organized, categorized, and ready for your accountant—or for your own tax preparation.

## Streamlining Customer Relationship Management

Your customers are the lifeblood of your business, but managing those relationships manually becomes impossible as you grow. Customer relationship management (CRM) automation keeps everyone organized and ensures no opportunity falls through the cracks.

**Automate lead capture**: Stop manually entering contact information from business cards, emails, or web inquiries. Modern CRMs can automatically capture leads from your website forms, social media, and even email signatures, creating a contact record without any manual data entry.

**Set up automated follow-ups**: Create email sequences that automatically follow up with prospects based on their actions. Someone downloaded your guide? Send them a related case study three days later. Had a sales call? An automatic follow-up email can go out the same afternoon while you move on to your next task.

**Track customer interactions automatically**: Many CRMs can log emails, phone calls, and meetings automatically, creating a complete history of your relationship with each customer. When you need to remember what you discussed last month, it's all there.

**Automate task creation**: Set your CRM to automatically create tasks for your team based on specific triggers. A new high-value lead comes in? A task is automatically assigned to your best salesperson. A customer hasn't been contacted in 60 days? A reminder task appears on the relationship manager's list.

## Automating Your Email Marketing

Email remains one of the most effective marketing channels for small businesses, but managing email campaigns manually is time-consuming and leads to inconsistent communication.

**Welcome new subscribers automatically**: When someone signs up for your mailing list, automatically send them a welcome email (or series of emails) introducing your business and offering immediate value. This creates a great first impression without requiring you to manually email each new subscriber.

**Segment your audience**: Automation tools can automatically sort your subscribers into different groups based on their behavior, interests, or characteristics. Customers in different segments receive different content—all automatically.

**Set up behavioral triggers**: Send targeted emails based on specific actions. A customer abandoned their shopping cart? Send a reminder email with a special offer. Someone clicked on a particular link? Send them more information on that topic. These timely, relevant emails perform much better than generic blasts, and they happen completely automatically.

**Schedule campaigns in advance**: Spend a few hours creating next month's email campaigns, schedule them, and move on. Your emails will send at optimal times even while you're serving customers or meeting with suppliers.

## Automating Social Media Management

Maintaining an active social media presence is essential for most small businesses, but posting consistently across multiple platforms can become a full-time job. Automation helps you maintain visibility without constant manual posting.

**Schedule posts in advance**: Dedicate an hour to creating and scheduling a week or month of social media posts. Tools like Buffer, Hootsuite, or Later allow you to queue up content across multiple platforms that will post automatically at the times you specify.

**Curate content automatically**: Set up RSS feeds to automatically share relevant industry content with your followers. You can review and approve suggestions or let certain trusted sources publish automatically.

**Automate cross-posting**: Publish once and automatically share across multiple platforms. While you should customize content for each platform, automation can help ensure you're maintaining a presence everywhere your customers are.

**Monitor mentions and keywords**: Automation tools can alert you when your business is mentioned online or when relevant keywords appear, allowing you to join important conversations without constantly searching.

## Streamlining Invoice and Payment Processing

Chasing payments is time-consuming and awkward. Automating your invoicing and payment process ensures you get paid faster with less effort.

**Generate invoices automatically**: Set up recurring invoices for subscription or retainer clients that send automatically each month. For project-based work, use templates that populate client information automatically—you just add the specific work items.

**Send automatic payment reminders**: Your system can send polite reminders when invoices are approaching their due date and follow-ups for overdue payments. This is often more effective than manual reminders because it's consistent and removes the personal awkwardness.

**Accept automated payments**: Set up automatic payment processing for recurring clients so they're charged automatically when invoices generate. This eliminates the back-and-forth of collecting payment and ensures consistent cash flow.

**Sync with your bookkeeping**: When your invoicing system connects with your bookkeeping software, incoming payments are automatically recorded and matched to the right invoices. No more manual entry or reconciliation of payment records.

## Automating Appointment Scheduling

If your business involves appointments, consultations, or meetings, you know how much time can disappear into back-and-forth emails trying to find a mutually convenient time.

**Let customers book directly**: Services like Calendly, Acuity, or Square Appointments allow customers to see your availability and book directly. They choose a time that works for both of you without a single email exchange.

**Send automatic reminders**: Reduce no-shows dramatically by automatically sending appointment reminders via email or text. Your system can send reminders 24 hours ahead, then again a few hours before the appointment.

**Automate follow-ups**: After an appointment, automatically send a thank-you email with next steps, request a review, or schedule the next appointment. This consistent follow-up improves customer experience and can drive additional revenue.

## Automating Inventory Management

For product-based businesses, inventory management can be incredibly time-consuming. Automation helps you maintain optimal stock levels without constant manual tracking.

**Track inventory automatically**: Modern point-of-sale systems automatically adjust inventory levels with each sale. You always know exactly what's in stock without manual counting or spreadsheet updates.

**Set up reorder alerts**: Configure automatic alerts when inventory falls below certain thresholds. You'll never run out of your best-selling products or discover too late that you're out of essential materials.

**Automate purchase orders**: Some advanced systems can even generate and send purchase orders automatically when inventory reaches reorder points, ensuring replenishment happens smoothly.

## Automating Team Communication and Project Management

As your team grows, keeping everyone aligned and informed becomes increasingly challenging. Automation can streamline internal operations significantly.

**Automate routine updates**: Use tools like Slack workflows or Zapier to automatically share important updates. When someone completes a project stage, relevant team members can be notified automatically. When a new customer signs up, the implementation team can be alerted immediately.

**Set up project templates**: If you regularly do similar types of projects, create templates with pre-defined tasks, assignments, and due dates. Starting a new project becomes a matter of clicking a button rather than recreating the entire workflow.

**Automate time tracking**: Time tracking software can automatically log time spent in different applications or on different projects, making timesheet completion effortless and ensuring accurate billing for hourly work.

## Getting Started with Automation

The prospect of automating your business might feel overwhelming, but the key is starting small and building momentum. Here's how to begin:

1. **Identify your biggest time drains**: Track your time for a week and identify which repetitive tasks consume the most hours.

2. **Start with one process**: Choose a single area to automate first. We'd recommend starting with bookkeeping or email marketing, as these typically offer quick wins with significant time savings.

3. **Choose user-friendly tools**: Don't overcomplicate things with enterprise software when simple solutions will do. Look for tools designed specifically for small businesses.

4. **Test and refine**: Automation isn't set-it-and-forget-it. Monitor your automated processes, gather feedback, and continuously improve them.

5. **Expand gradually**: Once you've successfully automated one area, move on to the next. Build your automation infrastructure piece by piece.
