## What Is Slack Frontiers?

Slack Frontiers is the company's premier annual event, bringing together business leaders, innovators, and forward-thinking professionals from around the globe. Think of it as part conference, part masterclass, and part inspiration session—all designed to help you reimagine how work gets done.

This isn't just another tech conference filled with buzzwords and product pitches. Frontiers 2021 focuses on real-world solutions to the challenges you face every day: keeping distributed teams connected, maintaining company culture remotely, automating repetitive tasks, and ensuring information flows seamlessly across your organization.

The event features keynote presentations from Slack executives and industry thought leaders, practical workshops that you can immediately apply to your workflows, customer success stories from brands you know and admire, and opportunities to connect with peers facing similar challenges in their organizations.

## Key Themes and Takeaways

Frontiers 2021 addresses the most pressing questions facing today's business leaders. Here are some of the central themes you can expect to explore:

### Building Your Digital Headquarters

The concept of a "digital headquarters" represents more than just communication software—it's a fundamental reimagining of where and how work happens. At Frontiers, you'll discover how leading organizations are creating digital spaces that rival the productivity and connection of physical offices while offering unprecedented flexibility.

This includes designing channel structures that mirror your organizational needs, creating spaces for both focused work and collaborative brainstorming, establishing norms that prevent digital burnout, and ensuring important information is accessible when and where people need it.

### Automation and Workflow Innovation

One of the most exciting frontiers (pun intended) in workplace productivity is automation. The right automated workflows can eliminate repetitive tasks, reduce human error, speed up approval processes, and free your team to focus on high-value work.

During the event, you'll see demonstrations of workflow automation in action, including real examples from companies that have automated everything from employee onboarding to sales handoffs. You'll learn which processes are prime candidates for automation and how to build workflows even if you're not a developer.

### Fostering Connection in Distributed Teams

Technology can connect us, but it takes intentionality to create genuine human connection across digital channels. Frontiers explores both the tools and the practices that help distributed teams build relationships, trust, and camaraderie.

You'll discover creative approaches to virtual team building, strategies for maintaining your company culture remotely, methods for encouraging spontaneous knowledge-sharing, and ways to ensure remote employees feel as included as their office-based colleagues.

### Security and Governance at Scale

As organizations centralize more work in digital platforms, security and governance become paramount. Frontiers addresses these concerns head-on, demonstrating how enterprise-grade security works in practice, how to maintain compliance in digital workspaces, strategies for managing data retention and privacy, and approaches to governance that provide oversight without sacrificing agility.

## How to Make the Most of Frontiers 2021

Attending Frontiers is valuable, but attending strategically is transformative. Here's how to maximize your experience:

**Come with specific challenges in mind.** Before the event, identify two or three pain points your organization is facing. This helps you filter sessions and ask targeted questions.

**Involve multiple stakeholders.** If possible, encourage colleagues from different departments to attend. The cross-functional perspective enriches your understanding and builds internal alignment.

**Take notes and plan action items.** The real value comes from implementation. Document ideas that resonate and create an action plan for bringing them back to your team.

**Engage with the community.** Frontiers attracts professionals facing similar challenges. The conversations you have with peers can be just as valuable as the formal sessions.

**Explore beyond your current use case.** Even if you're only using basic features today, see what's possible. The most transformative ideas often come from seeing capabilities you didn't know existed.
