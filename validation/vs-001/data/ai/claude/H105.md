# How Intrnz Boosted Annual Revenue by 250% with Pipedrive and Autopilot

When Sarah Chen founded Intrnz, a boutique recruitment platform connecting college students with internship opportunities, she had no shortage of ambition. What she did have a shortage of, however, was time, resources, and an efficient way to manage the complex dance of connecting candidates with employers.

Fast forward 18 months, and Intrnz has transformed from a struggling startup into a thriving recruitment powerhouse—with annual revenue growing by an astounding 250%. The secret? A strategic combination of Pipedrive and Autopilot that revolutionized how the company manages relationships, nurtures leads, and closes deals.

If you're wondering whether the right sales and marketing automation tools can truly transform your business, Intrnz's story provides compelling evidence that they can.

## The Challenge: Growing Pains Without the Right Systems

Like many small businesses, Intrnz started with the basics: spreadsheets, a generic email account, and plenty of manual follow-ups. Sarah and her small team of three were juggling relationships with university career centers, corporate HR departments, and thousands of student candidates—all while trying to close deals and generate revenue.

"We were drowning in data," Sarah recalls. "We'd have promising conversations with potential corporate clients, but then someone would forget to follow up. Student applications would sit in our inbox for days. We had no visibility into our sales pipeline, and our email marketing was essentially non-existent."

The problems compounded as Intrnz gained traction. The team was working 60-hour weeks yet somehow missing opportunities. Their conversion rates were abysmal—only 8% of interested companies actually became paying clients. Even worse, they had no systematic way to re-engage candidates or employers, meaning they constantly had to find new leads rather than nurturing existing relationships.

Something had to change.

## The Solution: Pipedrive for Sales Management

After researching various CRM platforms, Sarah chose Pipedrive for its intuitive interface and powerful sales pipeline management features. Unlike bloated enterprise solutions, Pipedrive offered exactly what Intrnz needed without overwhelming the team.

### Visualizing the Sales Process

The first breakthrough came from simply visualizing their sales pipeline. Using Pipedrive's customizable stages, the Intrnz team mapped out their entire sales process for corporate clients:

- Initial contact
- Discovery call scheduled
- Needs assessment completed
- Proposal sent
- Negotiation
- Contract signed

"Suddenly, we could see everything," Sarah explains. "We knew exactly where every potential client stood in our process. No more lost opportunities because someone forgot to send a proposal."

### Automating Sales Activities

Pipedrive's automation features eliminated hours of manual work every week. The team created automated workflows that:

- Assigned new leads to specific team members based on company size and industry
- Set automatic reminders for follow-up calls
- Moved deals through pipeline stages based on specific triggers
- Generated tasks when deals stalled in a particular stage

These seemingly simple automations had profound effects. Response times improved dramatically, and no lead fell through the cracks.

### Gaining Data-Driven Insights

Perhaps most valuable were Pipedrive's reporting capabilities. For the first time, Intrnz could track metrics that mattered:

- Average deal size
- Conversion rates at each pipeline stage
- Time to close
- Win/loss reasons
- Individual team member performance

These insights revealed bottlenecks the team hadn't even realized existed. For instance, they discovered that deals moved smoothly until the proposal stage, where conversion dropped by 40%. Armed with this knowledge, they revamped their proposal process, resulting in a 25% improvement in proposal-to-close rates.

## Adding Autopilot: Marketing Automation That Converts

While Pipedrive transformed Intrnz's sales process, they still faced challenges with marketing and lead nurturing. They needed a way to stay top-of-mind with both candidates and employers without manually sending thousands of emails.

Enter Autopilot.

### Creating Sophisticated Customer Journeys

Autopilot's visual canvas allowed the Intrnz team to design complex, multi-touch marketing campaigns that nurtured leads automatically. They created separate journeys for different audiences:

**For Student Candidates:**
- Welcome series introducing the platform
- Weekly internship opportunity digests
- Resume tips and career advice content
- Re-engagement campaigns for inactive users

**For Corporate Clients:**
- Educational content about hiring Gen Z talent
- Case studies and success stories
- Seasonal campaigns around peak hiring periods
- Upsell sequences for existing clients

These automated journeys meant that every contact received relevant, timely communications without any manual effort from the team.

### Behavior-Based Segmentation

One of Autopilot's most powerful features proved to be its ability to segment contacts based on behavior. Intrnz could now:

- Identify highly engaged students and fast-track them to employers
- Spot companies showing buying signals and alert the sales team
- Re-engage cold leads with targeted content
- Personalize communications based on past interactions

"The segmentation capabilities were game-changing," notes Marcus, Intrnz's head of marketing. "We went from batch-and-blast emails to highly personalized campaigns that people actually engaged with."

### Integration Magic

The real power emerged when Intrnz connected Pipedrive and Autopilot. This integration created a seamless flow of information between marketing and sales:

When a corporate contact engaged with marketing emails in Autopilot, that activity appeared in their Pipedrive record, giving the sales team valuable context for conversations. When deals closed in Pipedrive, contacts automatically entered onboarding sequences in Autopilot.

This closed-loop system ensured nothing fell through the cracks and every interaction was tracked and leveraged.

## The Results: Transformation by the Numbers

Eighteen months after implementing Pipedrive and Autopilot, Intrnz's results speak for themselves:

**Revenue Metrics:**
- 250% increase in annual revenue
- 34% improvement in sales conversion rates
- 28% increase in average deal size
- 3x increase in client lifetime value

**Efficiency Gains:**
- 15 hours per week saved on manual data entry and follow-ups
- 40% reduction in sales cycle length
- 67% improvement in email response rates
- 5x increase in marketing email engagement

**Growth Indicators:**
- Scaled from 3 to 12 team members
- Expanded from 2 to 15 university partnerships
- Grew corporate client base from 23 to 187 companies
- Increased active student users from 1,200 to 14,500

Perhaps most impressive: the team achieved these results without proportionally increasing headcount. The efficiency gains from automation allowed each team member to manage far more relationships and close more deals.

## Key Takeaways for Your Business

Intrnz's success story offers valuable lessons for any business looking to scale:

**1. Choose tools that match your maturity level.** Intrnz didn't need an enterprise CRM with endless features. They needed something intuitive that solved their immediate problems.

**2. Integration is essential.** The true power came from connecting sales and marketing systems, creating a seamless customer experience.

**3. Start simple, then iterate.** Intrnz didn't build complex automations overnight. They started with basic workflows and added sophistication over time.

**4. Data drives improvement.** Regular analysis of pipeline metrics and campaign performance enabled continuous optimization.

**5. Automation frees humans for high-value work.** By automating routine tasks, the team could focus on relationship-building and strategic thinking.
