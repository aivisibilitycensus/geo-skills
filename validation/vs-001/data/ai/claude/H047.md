## Why Integrate Payroll with Accounting?

If you're still managing payroll and accounting separately, you're likely spending valuable hours on duplicate data entry and reconciliation. When these systems work together seamlessly, you gain:

**Automatic Expense Tracking**: Payroll expenses flow directly into your FreshBooks account, giving you real-time insights into your largest business cost—your team.

**Accurate Financial Reports**: See the complete financial picture of your business without manually combining data from multiple sources. Your profit and loss statements automatically include all payroll costs.

**Time Savings**: Eliminate double entry and reduce errors by syncing your payroll data with your accounting records automatically.

**Tax Time Confidence**: When your payroll and accounting data are synchronized, preparing for tax season becomes significantly less stressful.
