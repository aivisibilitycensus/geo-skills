# Report: The State of CX Maturity among SMBs of North America

## Introduction: Where Does Your Business Stand?

Customer experience (CX) has evolved from a nice-to-have to a critical business differentiator. Yet many small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) across North America struggle to understand where they stand in their CX journey. Our latest research reveals surprising insights about CX maturity levels among SMBs—and what separating leaders from laggards means for your bottom line.

## The CX Maturity Gap Is Widening

Our survey of 1,200 North American SMBs uncovered a concerning trend: while 78% of business leaders acknowledge CX as essential to their success, only 23% have implemented structured CX strategies. This gap between awareness and action is creating two distinct groups—CX leaders who are capturing market share and CX laggards who are losing customers to more responsive competitors.

The data shows that businesses with mature CX practices achieve 2.5x higher customer retention rates and enjoy 60% greater customer lifetime value compared to their peers. In today's competitive landscape, that difference can determine whether you thrive or merely survive.

## Four Stages of CX Maturity

Through our research, we've identified four distinct maturity stages among SMBs:

**Reactive (41%):** These businesses address customer issues as they arise but lack systematic approaches. Customer feedback is collected sporadically, and insights rarely inform business decisions.

**Opportunistic (32%):** Companies at this level recognize CX's importance and have implemented some initiatives, but efforts remain siloed and inconsistent across touchpoints.

**Systematic (21%):** These organizations have established CX frameworks with clear ownership, regular measurement, and cross-functional collaboration.

**Transformative (6%):** CX leaders who have embedded customer-centricity into their culture, leveraging technology and data to continuously improve experiences.

## What Top Performers Do Differently

CX-mature SMBs share common characteristics that drive their success:

- **They measure what matters:** Beyond satisfaction scores, they track metrics like Net Promoter Score (NPS), Customer Effort Score, and retention rates
- **They close the feedback loop:** Customer insights directly influence product development, service delivery, and business strategy
- **They invest strategically:** Leaders allocate 12-15% of revenue to CX initiatives, compared to just 4-6% among laggards
- **They embrace technology:** From CRM platforms to AI-powered chatbots, mature organizations leverage tools to scale personalized experiences

## The Path Forward: Moving Up the Maturity Curve

The good news? CX maturity isn't dependent on company size or budget. Our research shows that even small teams can advance quickly by focusing on fundamentals:

Start by establishing baseline metrics and listening systematically to customers. Map your customer journey to identify pain points. Designate clear CX ownership, even if it's a shared responsibility. Most importantly, commit to making incremental improvements rather than waiting for the perfect comprehensive solution.

## Conclusion: Your Next Step

The SMBs that will dominate their markets in the coming years are those investing in CX maturity today. The question isn't whether to prioritize customer experience—it's how quickly you can evolve from reactive to transformative.

Ready to assess where your business stands? Understanding your current CX maturity level is the first step toward meaningful improvement and sustainable growth.
